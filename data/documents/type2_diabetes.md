# Type 2 diabetes handout

This is a sample education handout for the demo corpus. It is not a personal care plan.

## What type 2 diabetes is

Type 2 diabetes is a condition in which the body does not use insulin well, and over time may not make enough insulin. Glucose then stays higher in the blood than it should. It is different from type 1 diabetes, which is caused by autoimmune loss of the cells that make insulin.

This handout does not cover insulin doses, sliding scales, or how to adjust medicine.

## How monitoring is described here

A finger-stick or sensor glucose check shows the level at that moment. The HbA1c blood test reflects average glucose over about the previous three months.

Many adult education materials mention a general HbA1c target near 7 percent. The same materials say the target is individualized. Pregnancy, older age, and other illnesses change the goal. This handout does not assign a target to any person.

## High and low glucose

Symptoms that this handout associates with high glucose include unusual thirst, urinating more often, blurry vision, and tiredness.

Symptoms that this handout associates with low glucose include sweating, shakiness, fast heartbeat, hunger, and confusion. Low glucose is an urgent problem. The person should follow the low-glucose steps in their own care plan, then contact a clinician if symptoms do not improve.

## Food pattern used as an example

This handout's example plate is vegetables on half the plate, lean protein on one quarter, and high-fiber carbohydrate such as beans or whole grains on the last quarter. Sugary drinks are the item it says to cut first. It does not prescribe a calorie count.

## Sick days

When someone with type 2 diabetes is ill, this handout says to check glucose more often, drink fluids if they can keep them down, and call a clinician for vomiting, inability to drink, or glucose that stays very high. It says not to stop prescribed medicine because of this document.
