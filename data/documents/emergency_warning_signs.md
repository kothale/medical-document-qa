# Chest pain and emergency warning signs

This is a sample education handout for the demo corpus. It is not a triage tool and it cannot clear someone to stay home.

## Chest pain this handout treats as emergency

Call emergency services for chest pain or pressure that is new, severe, or lasts more than a few minutes, especially if it spreads to the arm, neck, jaw, or back. The same instruction applies when chest discomfort comes with shortness of breath, sweating, nausea, or feeling faint.

This handout says not to drive yourself if these symptoms are present.

## Other sudden symptoms

This handout also lists these as reasons to seek emergency care rather than wait for a clinic visit:

- One-sided weakness, facial droop, or trouble speaking
- Sudden severe headache
- Coughing or vomiting blood
- Severe allergic reaction with swelling of the lips or tongue, or trouble breathing
- A person who is unresponsive or not breathing normally

## What this handout will not do

It will not explain how to distinguish heartburn from a heart attack. It will not rank which hospital to choose. It tells the reader that new or severe chest pain is evaluated in person, because the cause cannot be settled from a document.
