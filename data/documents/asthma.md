# Asthma handout

This is a sample education handout for the demo corpus. It is not a personal care plan.

## What asthma does

Asthma is ongoing inflammation and narrowing of the airways. The muscles around the airways can tighten, and the lining can swell and make mucus. That makes it harder to move air.

Common symptoms in this handout are wheeze, cough, chest tightness, and shortness of breath. Symptoms often come and go. They may be worse at night or early in the morning.

## Triggers listed here

- Pollen, dust mites, and animal dander
- Tobacco smoke and strong fumes
- Cold air
- Exercise
- Viral respiratory infections
- Some medicines, such as certain anti-inflammatory drugs, if a clinician has already said they trigger that person's asthma

Avoiding known triggers is the prevention step this handout emphasizes.

## Two medicine roles, without doses

This handout describes two roles and does not name a dose:

- A reliever (rescue) inhaler is for sudden symptoms. It acts quickly and wears off.
- A controller medicine is taken on a regular schedule to reduce inflammation. It is not a substitute for emergency care when breathing is failing.

The written asthma action plan from a clinician is what tells that person when to use each medicine. This document cannot replace that plan.

## Emergency signs

Get emergency help if any of these happen: trouble speaking in full sentences, lips or fingernails turning blue or gray, ribs pulling in with each breath, or a reliever inhaler that is not helping. Do not wait for this handout to decide.
